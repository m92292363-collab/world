Questions

Things schools ask first.

Do we need fast internet to use it?

No. School OS installs like an app and is built for low-bandwidth places, so staff and students can use it on ordinary phones.

Who owns the website and the data?

Your school does. We host and maintain it for you, and your student records stay yours.

Can our own staff manage it?

Yes. Teachers enter marks and the admin handles registration and finance through simple panels, and we train your team at launch.

Do you work with schools outside The Gambia?

Yes. Everything is delivered remotely over WhatsApp, email and video call, so location is no barrier.

Can we start small and upgrade later?

Yes. Begin with a School Website and add portals, finance and clearance when you are ready.
